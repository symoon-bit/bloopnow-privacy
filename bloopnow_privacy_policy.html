Privacy Policy — BloopNow

Last updated: 21 August 2026

BloopNow ("we", "our", "the app") is a price-comparison app that helps you find and compare product prices across Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Meesho, and Nykaa. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

DataWhy we collect it
Name and email address (via Google Sign-In)To create and identify your account
Phone numberCollected once at sign-up to help secure your account and for order-related communication
Saved addressesSo you can save delivery addresses for your convenience within the app
Wishlist items and search historyTo show you your saved products and price comparisons across sessions

We do not collect payment information — all purchases are completed directly on the third-party platform (Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Meesho, or Nykaa) that you choose, outside of BloopNow.

How We Use Your Information

  • To operate core features: authentication, wishlist, saved addresses, and personalized price comparisons
  • To improve the app's functionality and reliability
  • To send you optional notifications about price drops or offers, if enabled

Third-Party Services We Use

  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Firestore Database) — to sign you in and securely store your account data
  • Groq API — to generate real-time price and rating estimates for the products you search
  • Unsplash — to fetch product images shown in the app
  • Affiliate networks (Amazon Associates, Cuelinks, EarnKaro) — when you tap "Buy Now," you are redirected to the retailer's website via a tracked affiliate link. We may earn a commission on qualifying purchases at no extra cost to you. These retailers have their own privacy policies governing the information they collect once you leave BloopNow.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only with the service providers listed above, strictly to operate the app's features.

Data Storage & Security

Your account data is stored securely using Google Firebase's Firestore database, which employs industry-standard security practices. We take reasonable measures to protect your data, but no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

Your Choices

  • You can edit or delete your saved addresses and wishlist items at any time from within the app
  • You can turn off notifications from the app's settings
  • You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us (see below)

Children's Privacy

BloopNow is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
